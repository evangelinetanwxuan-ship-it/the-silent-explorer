# The Silent Explorers

*Messages From Machines We Left Behind*: a NASA Space Apps 2026 game (Abandoned but Not Forgotten).

Visit the silent NASA machines left on the Moon, on Mars and in deep space. Brush away the dust, hear their stories, play their missions and learn the real facts.

**Play it here:** https://evangelinetanwxuan-ship-it.github.io/the-silent-explorer/

All photos are from NASA (images.nasa.gov). Aira, Grandpa, ECHO-7, Ben, Maya and Kofi are made-up characters; the machines, missions and facts are real. ECHO is named after NASA's real Project Echo balloon satellites (Echo 1 and Echo 2).
